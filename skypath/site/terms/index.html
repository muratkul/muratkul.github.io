SkyPath Terms of Service

Last updated: 7 July 2026

  1. License. SkyPath is licensed for personal, non-commercial use under the standard Apple Licensed Application EULA.
  2. Free version & ads. The free version shows non-personalized ads, including full-screen interstitials and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch. All free content can be completed without any purchase.
  3. Full Unlock. The one-time, non-consumable purchase permanently removes all ads and unlocks all premium content — premium airports, the full fleet and the mission modes — current and future, on your Apple ID. It is restorable on all your devices via Settings → Restore Purchases. Payments and refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  4. Fairness. Purchases never affect scoring; game timers always run in simulation time, so game speed never changes the outcome.
  5. Leaderboards. Scores must come from normal play; manipulated scores may be removed.
  6. Availability. The game is provided “as is”. Features may change; paid content is never removed while the app is available.
  7. Liability. To the extent permitted by law, the developer is not liable for indirect damages.
