Common questions

  • How do I remove ads? Buy Full Unlock — it removes all ads permanently and unlocks every premium airport, aircraft and mission mode, including future content.
  • I bought Full Unlock but don't see it. Open Settings → Restore Purchases (also on the store screen). Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID that made the purchase.
  • Do ads track me? No. Ads are non-personalized (contextual only); there is no App Tracking Transparency prompt and no cross-app tracking.
  • Where is my progress saved? On your device and, if you're signed in to iCloud, in your personal iCloud storage — so it follows your devices. There are no accounts.
  • Leaderboards / achievements? Handled by Apple Game Center. Sign in via iOS Settings → Game Center.
  • How do I request a refund? Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
