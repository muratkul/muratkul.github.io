# Privacy Policy

🌐 **English** · [Türkçe](./privacy-policy-tr)

_Last updated: November 1, 2026_

## 1. Introduction

Health Data Tracker & Widget ("Health Tracker", "the app", "we", "us") shows your daily
steps, walking + running distance and exercise minutes, with widgets, reminders and an
optional leaderboard. This policy explains, in plain language, how the app handles your
information.

The short version: your Apple Health data stays on your iPhone. If — and only if — you sign
in and turn on sharing for the leaderboard, a small weekly summary is stored on our server so
your friends (and, if you choose, everyone) can see it. You can stop sharing or delete your
account at any time from inside the app.

Questions? Contact us at murat.kul.dev@icloud.com.

## 2. Health Data on Your Device

With your permission, the app **reads** three types of data from Apple Health (HealthKit):

- Step count
- Walking + running distance
- Exercise minutes

The app **never writes** any data to Apple Health. This data is used on your device to show
your goals, trends, streaks, widgets and reminders. Detailed Health data (individual samples,
hourly or daily values) **never leaves your device** and is **never** stored in iCloud.

To show widgets, the app keeps a small daily summary (the totals above for the last few weeks)
and your goal settings on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

You can change or remove the app's Health access at any time in *Settings → Health → Data
Access & Devices → Health Tracker*.

## 3. Leaderboard (Optional)

The leaderboard is off until you sign in and turn sharing on. If you use it:

**Account.** You sign in with **Sign in with Apple**. We receive a user identifier and, if you
allow it, your first name and an email address (which may be an Apple private relay address).
Your first name is only used as a suggested display name; you can change it.

**What is shared.** While sharing is on, the app stores the following on our server:

- Your display name and your 6-character friend code
- For each week: your total steps, walking + running distance and exercise minutes, your
  average goal percentage and your current streak
- Your list of friends (people who added your code, or whose code you added)
- Whether you chose to appear on the global ("Everyone") board

**Who can see it.** Your friends can see your display name and weekly summary. If you turn on
"Show me to everyone", any signed-in user of the app can see your display name and weekly
summary on the global board. Nobody can see your Health data beyond this weekly summary.

**Reports.** If someone reports a display name on the global board, we store the reported name,
the reporter's user identifier and the time, so we can remove inappropriate names.

**Stopping and deleting.** "Stop sharing" removes this week's summary from all leaderboards and
stops new uploads. "Delete account" (Leaderboard → Settings) permanently deletes your profile,
friend code, friend links and all stored weekly summaries, and revokes Sign in with Apple.

## 4. Where Data Is Stored

Leaderboard data is stored with **Google Firebase** (Firebase Authentication and Cloud
Firestore), which acts as our service provider, in the European Union (europe-west region).
Data is encrypted in transit. See [Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## 5. What We Don't Do

- We do not use Health or leaderboard data for advertising, marketing or data mining.
- We do not sell or share data with third parties, other than storing it with Firebase as
  described above.
- No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
- The app is not directed at children under 13.

## 6. Notifications and Sharing

Reminders and "goal reached" alerts are **local notifications** created on your device. If you
tap **Share**, the app opens the standard iOS share sheet; nothing is sent unless you choose
where to send it.
