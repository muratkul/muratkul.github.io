# Privacy Policy

🌐 **English** · [Türkçe](./privacy-policy-tr)

_Last updated: October 31, 2026_

## 1. Introduction

Health Data Tracker & Widget ("Health Tracker", "the app", "we", "us") shows your daily
steps, walking + running distance and exercise minutes, with widgets and reminders to help
you reach your goals. This policy explains, in plain language, how the app handles your
information.

The short version: your Health data stays on your iPhone. We don't run any servers, we don't
collect anything, and we can't see your data.

Questions? Contact us at murat.kul.dev@icloud.com.

## 2. Health Data

With your permission, the app **reads** three types of data from Apple Health (HealthKit):

- Step count
- Walking + running distance
- Exercise minutes

The app **never writes** any data to Apple Health.

This data is used only on your device to show your goals, trends, streaks, widgets and
reminders. It is **not** sent to us or to anyone else, it is **not** stored in iCloud, and it
is **never** used for advertising, marketing or data mining.

You can change or remove the app's access at any time in *Settings → Health → Data Access &
Devices → Health Tracker*.

## 3. Data Stored on Your Device

To show widgets, the app keeps a small daily summary (the totals above for the last 31 days)
and your goal settings on your device, in a storage area shared only between the app and its
widgets. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## 4. Notifications

Reminders and "goal reached" alerts are **local notifications** created on your device. No
server is involved. You can turn them off in the app (Goals → Reminders) or in *Settings →
Notifications → Health Tracker*.

## 5. Sharing

If you tap **Share**, the app opens the standard iOS share sheet with a short message (for
example, "I hit my step goal today!"). Nothing is sent unless you choose where to send it.

## 6. What We Don't Do

- No account or sign-in
- No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs
- No selling or sharing of data with third parties
- No collection of data from children

## 7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
