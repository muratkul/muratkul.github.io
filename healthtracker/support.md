## Frequently Asked Questions

**The app shows no data.**
Allow Health access in *Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Health Tracker* and turn
on Steps, Walking + Running Distance and Exercise Minutes. Then open the app again.

**My widget says "Open Health Tracker to connect Apple Health".**
Widgets read the summary the app saves. Open the app once after installing; widgets update
automatically after that.

**Widgets don't update right away.**
iOS decides when widgets refresh. The app updates them whenever Apple Health has new data, but
there can be a delay of a few minutes. Health data is also locked while your iPhone is locked,
so updates continue after you unlock it.

**What does "On track" / "Behind" mean?**
It compares today with how much of your goal you usually have done by this time of day, based
on your last 30 days. "Behind · 20 min walk" means a 20-minute walk would put you back on track.

**I get too many / too few reminders.**
Go to *Goals → Reminders*. You can switch morning, midday and evening messages on or off, pick
the times, choose Fun or Calm messages and mute weekends. Each time slot sends at most one
message a day.

**Reminders arrive a bit late.**
iOS controls background activity, so a reminder can arrive later within its time slot.

**Is my Health data uploaded anywhere?**
No. Everything stays on your iPhone. See our [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy).
